## What it does

A complete, offline trophy guide and tracker for Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5: all 55 official trophies with their names, descriptions and grades, a multi-step guide for each one with every step's source cited, a roadmap to the platinum, and a tracker that saves your progress in your browser.

- **All 55 trophies** — 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, including the 28 hidden ones, with the official name, description and grade for each.
- **A real guide per trophy, in steps, with sources** — 170 cited guide steps in our own words, 56 of them backed by 2 or more outlets (PowerPyx, Exophase, Push Square, GTABase), counted live in the app. Steps whose method is still unconfirmed say so instead of guessing — including the one possibly missable trophy, Positive Mental Attitude, and the two trophies whose details are still unconfirmed.
- **Count trackers** — 24 grind trophies carry an X/Y counter (stunts, meditations, vaults, garages, laundered dollars and more), saved in your browser alongside the earned ticks.
- **Roadmap to the platinum** — a seven-stage plan from the story through side questlines, activities, collectibles, robberies and money, one-offs, and the 100% checklist, with an estimate of about 200 hours and per-stage progress.
- **Missable flags and timing grid** — each trophy is marked missable or not, with a dedicated Missable trophies tab that groups every timing warning by chapter and stage.
- **Sources tab** — a live source matrix (outlet × trophy) plus per-outlet coverage counts, computed from the guide data itself.
- **Tracker** — tick trophies as you earn them; progress is saved in localStorage and survives reloads. Filter by kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities, online) and by grade, search by name, and show earned-only.
- **Spoiler mode** — hides story trophy names and descriptions until you earn them.
- **Export / import** — save your progress (including counters) as a JSON file and restore it on any machine.
